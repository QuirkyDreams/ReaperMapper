THE IMPORTANT PART

History is observed. Today is modeled.

Historical Death Time Machine years use final annual mortality. ReaperMapper's TODAY display is a separate provenance mode built from provisional and final inputs. The two will never be presented as equivalent.

DATA STATUS

Why doesn't the slider have every year yet?

Because a smooth animation is not evidence. The complete target is 1968 → 2024 → TODAY, but a year enters production only after its death counts, age-adjusted rates, source era and state data pass validation.
