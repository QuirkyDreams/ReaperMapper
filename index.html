HOW DO YOU KNOW?

We don't watch deaths happen.

There is no national system reporting every U.S. death the moment it occurs. ReaperMapper therefore estimates expected mortality from official CDC/NCHS statistics. ≈ 8,273 is a modelled expectation, not a count of people observed dying today.

01 · NATIONAL ESTIMATE

Start with the newest credible annual picture.

Model 0.2 uses the CDC/NCHS 2025 provisional total of 3,094,593 U.S. deaths as its annual baseline. Provisional data are newer than final statistics, but can still be revised.

02 · SEASONALITY

Deaths are not evenly distributed through the year.

The annual baseline is distributed using the monthly pattern from the latest validated final mortality data. October's expected total is then divided across its 31 calendar days to produce today's daily expectation.

03 · STATES

Make the states add back to America.

Each state's daily estimate is allocated from the national estimate using its share of final 2024 U.S.-resident deaths. Integer allocation preserves the national total, so all 50 states plus D.C. sum to the displayed U.S. estimate.

04 · TWO MAP VIEWS

Rate and count answer different questions.

Mortality Rate colors states by CDC's final 2024 age-adjusted all-cause death rate per 100,000, making relative mortality easier to compare across states. Estimated Deaths Today colors the same map by Model 0.2's estimated daily state counts, showing where more deaths are expected in absolute numbers. Those counts are modelled—not observed live deaths.

SOURCES & DATA

Follow the numbers.

ReaperMapper separates official mortality data from the estimates built on top of it. The source year and status are shown wherever they matter.
